- **Living Sister Species Alignment (Approved Decision H5)**:
  - Mapped all 18 extinct taxa to their approved living sister counterparts (Mammoth $\rightarrow$ African elephant *Loxodonta africana*, Neanderthal/Denisovan $\rightarrow$ Modern human *Homo sapiens*, Dire Wolf $\rightarrow$ Gray wolf *Canis lupus*, Sabertooth/Cave Lion $\rightarrow$ Modern felid *Felis catus*, Steppe Bison $\rightarrow$ Bovine *Bos taurus*, Thylacine $\rightarrow$ Tasmanian devil *Sarcophilus harrisii*, Woolly Rhino/Elasmotherium $\rightarrow$ Horse *Equus caballus*, Dodo/Passenger Pigeon/Moa $\rightarrow$ Avian sister *Gallus gallus*, Glyptodon/Megatherium $\rightarrow$ Nine-banded armadillo *Dasypus novemcinctus*).

  - Queried Ensembl Compara REST API (`rest.ensembl.org`) with rate-limiting and raw disk caching in `data/raw_cache/orthology/`.

- **Relational Orthology Store & Full Coverage (§P8.3)**:
  - Initialized and populated table `extant_orthologs` in `data/paleo_metadata.db` with 5,627 rows.
  - Exported comprehensive coverage table to `data/orthology/coverage.csv` covering all 5,626 staged proteins + duplications.
  - Exported machine changelog to `data/changelogs/p8_orthology_changelog.jsonl` (5,627 entries).
- **Acceptance Verifications (§P8.4)**:
  - Re-verified a random sample of 25 ortholog records against live Ensembl endpoints with 25-second timeouts:
